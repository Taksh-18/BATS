About BATS (CSE3132 Lab)

Blood Availability Tracking System (BATS) connects hospitals, blood banks and donors in real time, with two differentiators:

  • HMS sync: issuing a unit pushes an event to the hospital's HMS (a mock HMS here).
  • Two-person verification: a second staff member must authenticate with their own password; the database also rejects issuer = verifier.
